# Art · passe 0.4.1

Création via l’outil intégré ImageGen, puis préparation WebP avec transparence conservée pour l’atlas. Les fichiers effectivement consommés par le jeu sont `public/valley-life-v1.webp` (1536 × 768, huit cellules) et `public/ruin-stone-v1.webp` (512 × 512). Les effets d’armes, runes, oiseaux et particules sont dessinés par le moteur, dans `app/class-art.ts`, `app/world-art.ts` et `app/valley.ts`.

## Prompt de l’atlas

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production environment sprite atlas for dark fantasy pixel-art first-person 2.5D RPG. Create ONE precisely aligned atlas, 4 columns by 2 rows, 8 equal square cells, each isolated object entirely inside its own cell with generous transparent margins, ground contact at same bottom baseline. No labels or grid lines. Actual transparent background. High detail hand-pixelled retro 16-bit art, natural muted green moss, weathered slate, warm amber light, crisp pixel clusters, consistent front three-quarter perspective nearly eye-level, gentle light from upper left. Objects in reading order: 1 mossy broken stone arch with trailing ivy; 2 gnarled ancient oak with hanging amber lantern; 3 wayside wooden shrine with candles and crimson cloth; 4 abandoned medieval camp tent with rolled blanket and cooking pot; 5 small cluster of pale wildflowers and ferns; 6 cluster of glowing amber mushrooms beside a mossy log; 7 rusty knight armor memorial with a ragged crimson banner; 8 old stone well with rope and wooden roof. Individual distinct silhouettes, no characters, no floor or scenery, no cast shadows outside object footprint, no text. Each cell is a separate game prop. Landscape canvas exactly 2:1 aspect.

## Prompt de la pierre

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: tileable wall texture for a premium retro pixel-art dark-fantasy RPG raycaster. One square flat orthographic texture, seamless on all four edges. Fill entire image with ancient grey-green limestone masonry: large irregular hand-hewn blocks in staggered courses, chipped bevels, fine cracks, tiny flecks of lichen and sparse moss in mortar. Damp weathered but not ruin rubble. Muted slate gray, warm sage, subtle ivory edge highlights. Carefully hand-painted 16-bit pixel clusters with high detail, no blur, consistent flat diffuse light so it can be engine-shaded. No directional cast shadows, no perspective, no ground, no frames, no symbols or text, no objects, no vines hanging across edges. Clean readable stone shapes, seamless tiling.
